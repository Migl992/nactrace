nactrace explain a cross-interface call on Etherlink / Tezos X

An EVM tx hash, a Tezos operation hash, or an explorer URL. The answer says which leg failed, why, and what was rolled back. Share the page URL: it carries the hash.

Examples: atomic revert · caught revert · nested Michelson → EVM → Michelson · mainnet withdrawal · shadownet revert

Also a CLI, a library, Hardhat and Foundry hooks, and this widget for your own pages (<script src=… data-hash=…>, see the demo). Source: github.com/Migl992/nactrace. Data: 0xTzKT and the public Etherlink nodes, read from your browser.
